Why Gammeleksgården

Built for the working week

The location by the E6

A short drive to industrial areas in Landskrona, Helsingborg and Lund, without sitting in city traffic.

Large car park

Room for service vans and trailers right outside the rooms, and an EV charging point.

Own kitchen

A shared kitchen where the team can cook dinner and make packed lunches.

The same room every week

Your employees get their room back every Monday. We can store belongings between weeks.

One invoice a month

A combined invoice with a breakdown per person and project. E-invoice via Peppol.

Cleaning as needed

Weekly cleaning and a change of bed linen are included. More frequent cleaning can be added.

How it works

From enquiry to key

  1. Enquiry

    Tell us how many rooms, which days of the week and for how long. It takes two minutes.

  2. Quote within a day

    You get a fixed price per room and night, with terms for cancellations and rebookings.

  3. Framework agreement

    The rooms are blocked on all channels for the whole period, so they are never sold to anyone else.

  4. Check-in

    Keys are collected at reception, or left out for you if you arrive late. You get an invoice at the end of the month.
