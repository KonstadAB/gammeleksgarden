Hire the party venue for a wedding, a 50th birthday or a company party. With 20 rooms in the same building, your guests don't need a taxi home, and the morning after you can all meet over coffee before everyone heads off.

Occasions

What are you celebrating?

Weddings

A ceremony at the farm, dinner in the venue and rooms for the bride and groom and the guests.

Birthdays

30, 50 or 80. A party all evening and your bed one flight of stairs away.

Business

Kick-off, Christmas buffet or conference with an overnight stay, close to the E6.

Family & clubs

Family reunions and club parties, with mini golf for the children.

How it works

One package, one contact

  1. Enquiry and viewing

    Send us the dates and number of guests. You're welcome to come and see the venue.

  2. Proposal and price

    Venue, rooms and extras in one quote. Your guests can book their rooms with their own code.

  3. The party

    You lay the tables and decorate the day before if the venue is free. A catering partner takes care of the food.
