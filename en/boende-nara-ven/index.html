Accommodation near Ven

Stay on the farm, cycle on Ven

Gammeleksgården is about a quarter of an hour from the Ven boat in Landskrona. Sleep well in the countryside, take the morning boat out and come back to a quiet room with a private bathroom.

The island in the middle of the Øresund

Why you'll want to go to Ven

Ven is a small island between Sweden and Denmark with rolling fields, steep cliffs down to the water and views across the whole sound.

  • Cycle around the island in a morning, stopping at the fishing villages
  • The Tycho Brahe museum and the remains of Uraniborg, where the astronomer had his observatory
  • The whisky and spirits distillery at Backafallen
  • Kyrkbacken, with its harbour, inn and St Ibb's church up on the cliff
  • Swimming and picnics on the beaches, with views towards Copenhagen and Kronborg

Here's how

A day on Ven from Gammeleksgården

  1. Drive to Landskrona

    About 10 km and a quarter of an hour from the farm to Skeppsbron. Park by the terminal.

  2. Take the boat

    Just under half an hour across to Bäckviken. Buy your ticket online from Ventrafiken in advance, especially in summer.

  3. Rent a bike

    Bikes can be rented at the harbour. The island is small enough to cycle around in a day.

  4. Back to the farm

    Have a round of mini golf in the evening sun and sleep well. Check-out isn't until 11 am.

Accommodation

A hotel near Ven, a quarter of an hour from the boat

Accommodation on Ven is small and runs out early in the summer. With us, you stay for a much lower price, with clean rooms, a private bathroom, free parking, mini golf and room for your motorhome. You don't have to catch the last boat back, and you're close to Helsingborg, Landskrona and the ferry to Helsingør.

We are on the mainland in Härslöv, not on the island itself.

Questions and answers

FAQ about Ven

Are there hotels on Ven?

Yes, but only a handful of small places to stay, and they fill up fast in summer. That's why many people stay on the mainland and take the boat over for the day.

How far is it from Gammeleksgården to the Ven boat?

The boat leaves from Skeppsbron in Landskrona, about 10 km from the farm and a quarter of an hour by car. There is parking right by the terminal.

How long does the boat to Ven take?

The crossing from Landskrona to Bäckviken on Ven takes just under half an hour. The boat runs all year round, several times a day. Buy your ticket online from Ventrafiken, otherwise there can be a long wait in summer.

Can I take the car to Ven?

You can, but we recommend leaving the car in Landskrona. The island is small and is best explored by bike. Bring your own bike on the boat for a fee, or rent one at the harbour in Bäckviken.

Why stay on the mainland rather than on the island?

You get quiet rooms with a private bathroom, free parking and a short drive to the E6. You can do Ven one day and Helsingborg, Landskrona or Kronborg the next, without having to catch the last boat back.
