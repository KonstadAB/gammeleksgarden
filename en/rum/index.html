Good to know

Breakfast and check-in

For breakfast you get a small package of tasty bits to eat in your room or take with you on the road. Collect your key at reception, which is open 08–22. Arriving later? Call or message us beforehand and we'll sort it out. When you check out, leave the key in the key drop box.

Smoking is only allowed in the designated area, well away from the windows and vents of the building.
