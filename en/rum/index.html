Gammeleksgården Hotel

Rooms & prices

20 rooms of approx. 20 m², all with a private bathroom and space for four. Every room has beds both downstairs and up in a loft. The room types differ only in how the beds are arranged.

Price per night: 1–2 guests from 795 kr, 3 guests from 995 kr and 4 guests from 1 195 kr. Prices vary by season.

Good to know

Check-in and food

Check-in from 5 pm and check-out by 11 am. Sometimes you can arrive earlier, but we can't promise it, so feel free to ask us. Pick up your key at reception: ring the bell and our staff, who live on the farm and normally answer 08–22, will come. If you're arriving later, call or write to us beforehand and we'll sort it out. When you check out, leave your key in the key drop box.

Breakfast isn't served at the moment, but it usually is in summer. In the shared kitchen you can cook your own food and keep things in the fridge, around the clock.

Pets are not allowed in the hotel rooms.

Smoking is only allowed in the designated area, well away from the windows and vents of the building.
