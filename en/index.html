Härslöv · between Landskrona and Helsingborg

A countryside hotel right by the E6with rooms for the whole family

Gammeleksgården has 20 clean, quiet rooms with private bathroom, motorhome pitches, a party venue for 80 guests and its own mini golf course. Five minutes from the motorway and a quarter of an hour from both Landskrona and Helsingborg.

What would you like to do at Gammeleksgården?

Hotel

Stay overnight on your journey along the E6, visit Ven and Helsingborg or stay close to family. Rooms for 1–4 people and an RV pitch for your motorhome.

Rooms and prices

Business

Accommodation for fitters, tradespeople and consultants. Rent 5–20 rooms in a block, week by week, at a fixed price with one combined invoice.

Request a quote

Parties & weddings

A party venue for 80 seated and 120 standing, with rooms so your guests can stay the night. Weddings, 50th birthdays and club parties.

Plan your party

Mini golf

18 holes on the farm. Included for hotel guests and open to everyone in season. A perfect stop with the kids.

The location

In the middle of north-west Skåne, five minutes from the E6

Peaceful countryside, but close to everything. Take the boat to Ven from Landskrona, the ferry to Helsingør from Helsingborg, or carry on south towards Malmö and Copenhagen.

  • Landskrona centreapprox. 9 km
  • Helsingborg centreapprox. 18 km
  • The boat to Ven (Landskrona)approx. 10 km
  • Malmöapprox. 45 km
  • Copenhagen Airportapprox. 75 km
