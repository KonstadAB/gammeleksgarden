Härslöv · between Landskrona and Helsingborg

A countryside hotel right off the E6with room for the whole family

20 clean, quiet rooms with private bathrooms, RV pitches, a party venue for 80 guests and our own minigolf course. 15 minutes from Landskrona and Helsingborg, under an hour from Copenhagen Airport.

Rooms

Double rooms and family rooms with loft beds for up to four. Self check-in with a door code, free parking and EV charging.

Parties & weddings

Venue for 80 seated or 120 standing guests, with rooms on site so everyone can stay the night.

Business stays

Block bookings of 5–20 rooms for crews and consultants, with fixed rates and monthly invoicing.
