Gammeleksgården Mini golf

18 holes of mini golf in the countrysidefree for our guests

A peaceful course at the farm, a few minutes from the E6. Play a round with the kids before dinner or in the evening sun.

Free for our guests

If you're staying at the hotel or the RV pitches, you can play as much as you like, free of charge.

You can borrow clubs and balls at reception or from the box by the course.

Hotel, RV pitches and a party venue in the countryside between Landskrona and Helsingborg.
